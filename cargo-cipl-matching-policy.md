# Cargo & CIPL Accuracy Policy

## 1. Customer Obligation

The customer must make sure that the cargo shipped matches the Commercial Invoice & Packing List (CIPL) exactly. This covers, without limitation, the description of the goods, quantities, weights, dimensions, packaging, markings, HS codes and declared values.

Any photos, documents or other details the customer gives us must accurately show the actual cargo being shipped.

## 2. Why This Matters

Shipments are regularly held by Customs when the details given to us (in photos or documents) do not match the actual cargo. These mismatches cause problems at the point of inspection and lead to the goods being detained.

## 3. Holds Caused by Cargo / CIPL Mismatch: Customer Responsibility

If a shipment is held, detained or delayed because the actual cargo does not match the CIPL or the information the customer gave us:

- the matter is **outside our scope of responsibility**; and
- **all costs that result will be borne by the customer**, including but not limited to storage, demurrage, detention, inspection, re-documentation, penalties, fines and any extra handling or transport charges.

## 4. Holds for Any Other Reason: Our Responsibility

If a shipment is held for **any reason other than** a cargo / CIPL mismatch, the shipment **remains our responsibility** until the goods are released and delivered to the customer, **at no additional cost to the customer**.

## 5. Acknowledgement

By booking a shipment with us, the customer accepts the terms of this policy.
